MealWise Food Extractor v1 extracts food names, quantities, and units from
meal descriptions. It is the selected step-1000 Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct LoRA
fine-tune, fused and exported to Q4_K_M GGUF.

The model file is 397,807,456 bytes. Its SHA-256 is:

```text
4cff339c4ed40d6e00742c46c34939d08b62f845c70faf4c4ed1e512b34b27e9
```

MealWise resolves nutrition through its reference database and keeps inferred
meals available for review. Extracted food names and portions may be incorrect.

The release includes the Apache-2.0 license, upstream attribution, a pinned
model manifest, and a checksum file.
